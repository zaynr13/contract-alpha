# Data notice

The source websites and their data remain subject to their respective terms, licenses, and attribution requirements. This repository does not grant rights to FanGraphs, Sports Reference, Spotrac, Steamer, MLB, or third-party data.

The hosted application reads only committed transformed research outputs: aggregate estimates, validation summaries, market-rate estimates, and short derived leaderboards. It makes no source-site requests and trains no models at runtime. The complete contract-level research panel and timelines are generated under `data/processed/private/` and excluded from git pending redistribution permission. This narrower design reduces redistribution risk but does not grant new data rights.

FanGraphs' current Terms of Service restrict unauthorized reproduction or publication of its content in whole or in part. Before publishing the complete row-level dataset, enabling live ingestion, or commercializing a derived product, review the then-current terms and obtain and document any permissions required for automated access, derived products, caching, and redistribution.
