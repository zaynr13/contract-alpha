# Data notice

The source websites and their data remain subject to their respective terms, licenses, and attribution requirements. This repository does not grant rights to FanGraphs, Sports Reference, Spotrac, Steamer, MLB, or third-party data.

Only aggregate feasibility outputs are committed in Phase 1. The Phase 1 dashboard reads those committed aggregates, makes no source-site requests at runtime, and does not expose player-level records. This narrower design reduces redistribution risk but does not grant new data rights.

Before publishing a row-level dataset, live-ingestion product, or model-backed application, obtain and document the permissions required for automated access, derived products, caching, and redistribution.
