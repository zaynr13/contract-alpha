# Phase 2 research outputs

These files are generated by `scripts/run_phase2.py` and are the only Phase 2 inputs read by the hosted app.

- `analysis_summary.json`: cohort, headline estimates, validation summary, method, and limitations.
- `research_results.csv`: coefficient estimates, clustered uncertainty, and sensitivity specifications.
- `model_validation.csv`: expanding-window test folds for AAV, guarantee, and contract years.
- `market_price_per_war.csv`: offseason-specific market valuation series.
- `team_results.csv`: signing-team primary-cohort cost, production value, and realized alpha aggregates.
- `leaderboards.json`: short transformed top-ten research tables.

The complete contract-level research panel and player timelines are written to `data/processed/private/`, which is git-ignored pending source-data redistribution permission. The hosted app does not scrape or retrain at runtime.
